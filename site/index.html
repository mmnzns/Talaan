Meeting notes that stay on your computer.

Talaan records your calls and writes the notes for you, without a bot joining the meeting. Your recordings and transcripts never leave your computer, and the summaries can be written there too.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

No account. No subscription. Works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp and more.

how it works

Three steps, and the first one is just your call.

  1. 1

    Join your call as usual

    Use Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack or whatever you already use. Nothing to install in the call, and nobody gets an invite from a bot.

  2. 2

    Talaan notices and records both sides

    When a call app starts using your microphone, Talaan asks if you want to record. It records you and the other people on the call.

    zoom is using your mic

    Record this call?

    Everything stays on your computer.

    RecordNot now
  3. 3

    Get a transcript and a summary

    When the call ends, you get the full transcript and a short summary: what was decided, who does what by when, and what's still open. Click any line to hear that moment again.

what you get

Notes you can actually use.

  • Who said what

    Your voice is "Me", the call is "Them". Talaan can also tell speakers apart, and you rename them once.

  • Decisions and to-dos

    Each summary lists decisions with the time they were made, and action items with an owner and a due date.

  • Ask your meetings

    Ask a question across all your meetings and get an answer with links to the exact moment.

  • Share it your way

    Copy the notes as text, save a PDF, or send them to Notion. Fix a word in the transcript any time.

privacy

Your meetings are yours.

Talaan works on your computer, even with Wi-Fi off once it's set up. Nothing about your meetings leaves your computer unless you choose to send it.

  • Local by default. Recording, the transcript and the summary all happen on your computer.
  • No account. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.
  • No bot. Nobody joins your call. Talaan gives you a short message you can paste in the chat to let people know.
  • You choose the cloud. If you want, you can have summaries written by a cloud AI service such as Claude, OpenAI or Gemini, using your own key. Only the transcript text is sent, never the audio.
  • No tracking. Talaan doesn't collect usage data or analytics.

Your computer will probably warn you. That's expected.

Talaan is free and made by one person. It isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, so your computer can't vouch for it and asks you first. Everything is built from the public source code, which anyone can read.

On Windows

  1. If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, choose Keep. In Edge: ... › Keep › Show more › Keep anyway.
  2. Double-click the installer. If you see Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. The publisher shows as Unknown publisher. That's expected, and it only happens once.

On a Mac

macOS blocks Talaan twice: once when you open the .dmg, and once the first time you open the app. Each time:

  1. Click Done. Not Move to Trash.
  2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. Enter your Mac password, then click Open Anyway again.

You only do this once. Updates open normally.

questions

Good questions.

Is Talaan really free?

Yes. Talaan is free and open source, with no account, no trial and no subscription. The only thing that could cost money is optional: if you choose to use a cloud AI for summaries with your own key, that company may charge you for it.

Do the other people on the call know I'm recording?

No bot joins, so they won't see one. When you record, Talaan gives you a short notice you can paste in the call chat with one click. It's good manners to tell people, and in some places the law requires it.

Which call apps does it work with?

Any app that uses your microphone for calls, including Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack huddles, Webex, Discord, WhatsApp and FaceTime on the Mac. You can set each app to ask first, always record or never record. Dictation apps never trigger it.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, once it's set up. The first time, Talaan downloads its speech and summary models (only when you click Download). After that, recording, transcripts and summaries all work offline.

What languages does it understand?

The most accurate speech model understands 99 languages, including Filipino. A faster one, recommended on Windows, covers 25 European languages. You can switch any time in Settings.

Where are my meetings saved, and can I delete them?

Meetings are plain files in a folder on your computer, readable even without Talaan. Audio is kept for 7 days by default, then deleted (you can change this, or pin a meeting to keep it). Settings › Privacy › Delete all Talaan data removes everything.
